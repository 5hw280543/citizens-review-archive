## 3. Granular Breakdown of Misinterpretations vs. Hard Evidence

### A. The "Fixed Plant" Fallacy (Design Spec vs. Field Operations)
* **Incorrect Narrative:** The 2017 Phase 3 upgrade resolved structural pollution, rendering the modernized plant non-polluting.
* **Factual Correction:** Structural capital completion does not equal operational compliance. The British Columbia Ministry of Environment issued formal Non-Compliance Advisories post-upgrade in 2018 (`IR76276`), 2019 (`IR115167`), and 2021 (`IR163340`). In June 2020, mechanical failure of the primary filtration system necessitated a full bypass of secondary biological treatment, discharging unpolished effluent directly into the marine outfall.

### B. Theoretical vs. Actual Performance Metrics
* **Incorrect Narrative:** Citing static 94% organic reduction, 98% TSS removal, and 98% BOD reduction as continuous operational performance.
* **Factual Correction:** Metrics cited in engineering summaries represent peak design capacity under ideal baseline conditions. Field inspections show recurring parameter exceedances for Biological Oxygen Demand ($BOD_5$), out-of-range chlorine residuals, and missed underwater outfall inspection schedules.

### C. Conflation of Legacy Industrial Toxins with Active Pathogen Discharges
* **Incorrect Narrative:** Attributing harbour water quality degradation solely to legacy coal/smelter brownfields and low tidal flushing.
* **Factual Correction:** While historical industrial pollutants remain in benthic sediments, modern regulatory interventions by DFO target active biological hazards. DFO emergency shellfish closures in Area 17 (recorded across 2018, 2020, 2021, and August–September 2023) are categorized as **Sanitary Contamination Closures** directly caused by human waste and active WWTP outfall loading.

### D. Inflow and Infiltration (I&I) vs. External Stormwater
* **Incorrect Narrative:** Presenting heavy rain runoff as isolated surface water flowing through external creeks.
* **Factual Correction:** Severe weather introduces extreme Inflow and Infiltration into the town's sanitary collection lines. The hydraulic surge exceeds WWTP volume capacity, triggering emergency overflow routing and unpermitted raw effluent discharges (`IR163340`).

### E. Shellfish Bed Viability and Remediation Scope
* **Incorrect Narrative:** Asserting that waterfront brownfield remediation has successfully restored the native shellfish harvesting economy.
* **Factual Correction:** Upland and intertidal soil remediation (24 municipal acres, 68 Crown acres) addresses legacy heavy metals, not microbiological contamination. Shellfish harvesting remains closed; Stz'uminus First Nation leadership records confirm persistent pathogen levels have prevented safe bivalve consumption for decades.
